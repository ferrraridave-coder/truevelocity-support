1. Description of the App

True Velocity is a GPS-based speedometer and trip recorder for iPhone. It displays real-time speed using your device's built-in GPS, and lets you optionally record trips that are stored locally on your device.

Key features include:

  • Analogue speed dial
  • Large digital speed display
  • MPH and KMH unit selection
  • Real-time GPS accuracy display
  • Three user-selectable leather-grain backgrounds (Black, Oxblood, and Tan)
  • Optional, user-initiated trip recording with on-device summary (duration, distance, average and top speed)
  • Pause and resume trip recording mid-journey
  • Optional Map Visualization to view recorded routes on an interactive map (stores GPS coordinates locally on your device)

The App works without requiring an internet connection. Speed calculations and trip statistics are performed locally on your device. Map Visualization uses Apple Maps, which may require an internet connection to load map tiles.

4. Trip Recording

Trip recording is optional. It only runs after you have enabled Log Trips in the Trips screen (which requires granting Always-location permission so trips remain accurate when the screen locks) and have explicitly tapped the record button on the main screen to start a trip. You may pause and resume recording at any time during an active trip. While a trip is in progress, the App may use location services in the background so the trip remains accurate if your screen locks or you switch apps. Background location use ends as soon as you stop the trip.

If you enable Map Visualization in the Trips settings, the GPS coordinates of your route are saved locally on your device as part of each trip record, so the route can be displayed on a map. This feature is off by default. Route coordinates are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to the developer, to iCloud, or to any third party.

Trip summaries and any associated route data are stored only on your iPhone. You are responsible for any backup of trip data; uninstalling the App will permanently delete your trip history. The App applies minimum duration and distance thresholds and will discard recordings that do not meet them.
